Quick wins for this week

Things you can do now

Small changes that start working straight away.

01

Swap the stock photos for your own

You already have gorgeous photos of your space and a walk-through video. Put them on the homepage and treatment pages, and add a photo of you and the team. Clients want to see who they're about to meet.

02

Write it the way you'd say it

Read each page out loud. If you wouldn't say it to a client over a cuppa, rewrite it. "Leaking when you laugh?" works because it's how people actually talk.

03

Add an honest-answers section

Do I get undressed? Does it hurt? How many sessions? Answer the questions people are too shy to ask, in plain words, on the homepage.

04

Put your Meta pixel to work

Your pixel is already installed. Build audiences from your website visitors and video viewers, so everyone who has looked at EMSELLA sees your next story.

05

Ask every happy client for a Google review

You're on 5.0 from 19 reviews. An automatic thank-you text after each visit, with a direct link, turns that into a steady stream.

06

Ask your agency for the logins

Before anything changes, make sure you own your ad accounts, pixel, Google tag and audience data. That makes the move in-house painless whenever you're ready.

Wording that stays inside the rules

Device advertising has strict rules about results and statistics. A few lines from the current site, rewritten so they stay warm and stay compliant.
