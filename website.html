Honest answers

The questions you're too polite to ask

Straight answers, no jargon. Anything else, just ask us. Honestly, we've heard it all.

Ask us yourself

Do I have to get undressed?

Nope. For EMSELLA you sit on the chair fully clothed, then carry on with your day. It's the one our clients call "the magic chair".

What does EMSCULPT actually feel like?

Strong muscle contractions, far more than you could do on your own. A bit like the feeling after a really good workout, without the sweaty gym clothes.

Is there downtime?

No downtime to plan around. Park out the front, pop in for your session and head straight back to your day.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on you and what you're working on. Our registered nurse maps it out at your consultation, so you know exactly what you're signing up for before you start.

Is it right for me?

That's what the consultation is for. We'll tell you honestly if it's a good fit, and if it isn't, we'll tell you that too.

Meet the machines

Clever technology, explained like a friend would

  • EMSELLAThe chair. You sit, fully clothed, while it works the deep pelvic floor muscles that are hard to reach on your own.
  • EMSCULPTFor muscle and body shape. Powerful contractions that go well beyond what a workout can do.
  • EXIONFor skin. A 3-in-1 platform using radiofrequency, with or without microneedling, planned around your skin.

All three are made by BTL Aesthetics. Your registered nurse talks you through suitability, risks and what to expect before any treatment.
